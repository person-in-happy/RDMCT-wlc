# JMS manuscript review workflow

Source: `JMS_Review_CN.tex` in this directory. Preserve a dated original before substantial revision, and keep a change record. Do not replace the source with older copies in `../JMS/`.

Read the manuscript and trace substantive claims to source code, immutable instance/checkpoint hashes, run configuration and validation records. Use current journal and original-paper sources for external claims. Flag unsupported industrial provenance, online deployment and statistical significance instead of writing them as facts.

For experiment comparisons, align objective boundary, recipe, sample count, solver/training seed, time budget, warm-start provenance and second-stage status. Keep old makespan including activated cleaning unloading separate from product-return makespan. Offline SPBS construction cost is distinct from the main solve budget.

For active campaigns, inspect logs/checkpoints without changing inputs. Determine campaign coverage and objective from its frozen configuration rather than older review notes. Pending results stay pending in the manuscript.

The November 25 reviewer revision confirms independent load-lock slots and permits a full-chamber half-turn at an emptying boundary to unload a rear product pair before a front auxiliary pair. Use the [extended Pjob model](experiments/terminal_unload_20261125/extended_model.py) for that equipment rule. The earlier grid, Pareto certificates and model hashes below belong to the front-first protocol. Their recorded feasible curves remain reproducible under that protocol. The 7-second terminal residence bound and its derived objective bound cannot be transferred to the union of both unload orders. The [new matched-cap comparisons](results/terminal_unload_20261125/scope/summary.json) and [orientation replay](results/terminal_unload_20261125/event_replay.json) validate the extended model. For the current manuscript, see the [revision and reproduction index](review/reviewer_20261125/README_CN.md).

For experiment execution, use the `experiment-runner` defaults for resumption and wall-time estimates. The Chinese submission uses mean product residence plus the square of the maximum within-recipe spread divided by twice a one-second time scale, plus one equivalent second per unused production cycle immediately before an actual cleaning. The final uncleaned segment contributes zero. The current [score and original-model solver](experiments/square_residence_20261125/solver.py) implement this objective. The [publication audit](tools/verify_square_experiments_20261125.py) traces the three tables and four figures to physical schedules.

For the [108 threshold combinations and scoped timing experiments](results/square_grid_20261125/validation.json), reuse a returned schedule only if its newly measured score and a valid lower bound meet the requested gap. The [seven single- and two-objective comparisons](results/square_table4_20261125/publication.json) and the [paired ablations](results/square_residence_20261125/table5/table5_seeds.csv) retain the original seeds, configurations and stopping rules. The exceptional paired comparator can be resumed in the foreground with [resume_table5.ps1](experiments/square_residence_20261125/resume_table5.ps1). The [displayed continuous Pareto branches](results/pareto_publication_20261125/displayed_curves.json) and their [independent replay and archive-dominance audit](results/pareto_publication_20261125/validation.json), the [fixed-order supply bounds for the 75-wafer figure](results/square_grid_20261125/gantt75_gap/absolute_event_bounds.json), the [Pareto certificates](results/pareto_square_20261125/certificate.json) and [sensitivity checks](results/square_residence_20261125/sensitivity.json) are computational records. Keep all front-verification material outside the submission. For historical records, the [piecewise residence campaign](experiments/huber_residence_20261125/resume.ps1), the [linear spread campaign](experiments/residence_objective_20261125/resume.ps1) and [November 24 campaign](experiments/table45_20261124/resume.ps1) retain their own protocols.

After each campaign finishes, verify exit status and expected instance coverage. Independently validate available solutions under the correct model/goal; distinguish optimal, feasible timeout, no-incumbent timeout and proven infeasible. Use matched objectives and models for mode ordering. Exact values establish ordering, and a validated feasible upper bound below the competing modes’ proven optima also establishes dominance. If certification is incomplete, report bounds and identify only the ordering that remains unresolved; never infer infeasibility from timeout. Do not replace fixed-budget algorithm benchmarks with long-budget retry results.

Compile the revised Chinese source with XeLaTeX, resolve references, check equations and rendered pages, and report actual validation. Deliver the source, PDF, review findings and deferred-evidence list. A finished process alone does not imply a complete experiment. Do not promise unattended future monitoring unless a real persistent mechanism was installed and verified.
