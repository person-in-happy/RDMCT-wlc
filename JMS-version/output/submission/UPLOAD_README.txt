JOURNAL OF MANUFACTURING SYSTEMS
Submission files prepared 26 November 2026

JMS_Submission_EN.pdf is the English review manuscript. It includes the author names and affiliation, the corresponding author's address and email, six keywords, editable-source equations and tables, figure captions, funding, competing interests, author contributions, data availability, and the declaration of AI-assisted manuscript preparation.

JMS_LaTeX_Source.zip contains the manuscript and its nine vector artwork files at one folder level. Extract the archive and compile JMS_Submission_EN.tex with pdfLaTeX three times. The bibliography is embedded. Figure3a.pdf and Figure3b.pdf are the two panels of manuscript Figure 3. If the submission system asks for individual figures, the identically named PDF files are also supplied separately.

Supplementary_Data.zip accompanies the manuscript. It contains the threshold settings, objective values, bounds, timing fields and stopping records for 108 cases, the two 60-epoch training logs, checkpoint identifiers and an English file guide.

Highlights.docx contains four editable highlights. Cover_Letter.docx is addressed to the journal's editors. Title_Page.docx and CRediT_Statement.docx reproduce the author information and roles in the manuscript. Declaration_of_Interest.docx uses Elsevier's Word template with the authors' no-known-interests declaration selected. Submission_Metadata.txt supplies the title, abstract and other submission fields.

JMS_Submission_EN.zip is the delivery bundle. Extract it to select individual files for the journal's upload fields. The 2024 journal guide asks for five potential referees with addresses, email addresses and reasons for suitability. The corresponding author must confirm their eligibility and conflicts and provide the names in the live submission system if requested. The author also confirms any portal attestations and the preference for color in print.

The journal's archived Guide for Authors identifies single-anonymized review, numbered references in order of first citation, up to six keywords, and separate highlights. Its public submission portal linked to the journal guide on 26 November 2026, but the live guide returned HTTP 403. The journal-specific checks here use the official guide as archived on 14 April 2024. Elsevier's current public LaTeX, Highlights, and AI policies were checked separately.
