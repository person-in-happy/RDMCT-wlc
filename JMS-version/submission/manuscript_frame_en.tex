\title{Bi-objective scheduling of dual-source mixed-flow rotating-chamber cluster tools under full-occupancy processing}
\author[ustb]{Lincong Wei}
\author[ustb]{Lizhen Shao\corref{cor1}}
\ead{lshao@ustb.edu.cn}
\author[ustb]{Wei Wang}
\cortext[cor1]{Corresponding author.}
\address[ustb]{Department of Control Science and Engineering, University of Science and Technology Beijing, No. 30 Xueyuan Road, Haidian District, Beijing 100083, China}
\begin{abstract}
Advanced semiconductor manufacturing requires high throughput and complex process recipes. Schedules for rotating-chamber cluster tools must balance batch makespan and process quality within a limited computation time. All four chamber positions must be occupied during processing. The same physical auxiliary wafers fill unused positions in production and occupy the chamber during cleaning. Their loading and unloading order affects both the time at which product wafers return to their load ports and how long they wait in the chamber, making it harder to allocate resources and sequence robot operations. We formulate a bi-objective mixed-integer model that minimizes batch makespan and a process objective combining mean nonprocessing residence time, within-recipe variation in residence time, and the shortfall from the chamber contamination threshold at each cleaning, measured in production cycles. For batches of 2, 4, and 8 product wafers split equally between two recipes, the schedules attain the lower bounds on both objectives simultaneously. For the 16-wafer mixed batch, two-stage optimization reduces the mean process objective by 65.14\% relative to makespan-only optimization at the same makespan of 810~s. In the four-wafer mixed-batch ablation, mean makespan falls from 403~s with the hierarchical sequence model (HEM) cut-selection baseline to 398~s with the proposed method. Removing hierarchical refinement of the initial schedule increases the mean to 406.33~s. Pareto fronts show which objective pairs dominate others, and Gantt charts show when wafers occupy each resource.
\end{abstract}
