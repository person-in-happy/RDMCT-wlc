Bi-objective scheduling of dual-source mixed-flow rotating-chamber cluster tools under full-occupancy processing

Full-occupancy processing couples production and cleaning in dual-source mixed-flow rotating-chamber cluster tools because both operations draw on the same stock of reusable auxiliary wafers. We formulate a bi-objective mixed-integer model that minimizes the time until all product wafers return to their original load ports (makespan) and a process objective that accounts for nonprocessing chamber residence, within-recipe variation in residence time, and unused production cycles before cleaning. We encode schedules by batch task and wafer pair, construct and improve a feasible starting schedule, and decode the solution into individual wafer movements. The two-stage algorithm RL-DMCT extends the hierarchical sequence model (HEM) for cut selection with variable-role features and candidate-cut reranking. It first searches for a shorter makespan, then improves the process objective within the makespan bound and discrete structure passed to the second stage. For equal-mix instances with two, four, and eight product wafers, a single schedule attains both lower bounds in each instance. For the 16-wafer mixed instance, the mean process objective falls by 65.14% relative to makespan-only optimization at the same 810 s makespan. In the four-wafer mixed-instance ablation, the mean makespan is 403 s with HEM and 398 s with RL-DMCT. Removing hierarchical improvement of the starting schedule raises it to 406.33 s. Pareto fronts characterize objective dominance, and Gantt charts show when products and auxiliary wafers occupy shared resources.
