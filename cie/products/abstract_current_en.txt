PW-pair-based two-stage MILP scheduling and structured solution methods for dual-source mixed-flow rotating-chamber cluster tools

Full-load processing in dual-source mixed-flow rotating-chamber cluster tools requires reusable auxiliary wafers to fill vacant chamber positions and support cleaning. We formulate a two-stage mixed-integer linear program using two-wafer pairs to connect handling operations and chamber cycles, and batch inventory variables to represent interchangeable products. The model jointly determines pairing, chamber assignment, cleaning, auxiliary-wafer reuse, and resource sequences. The first stage minimizes product-return makespan; the second refines waiting and cycle regularity while retaining the primary upper bound and main discrete structure. RL-DMCT combines structured initialization, five-level binary-skeleton improvement, and feature and inference extensions to HEM cut selection. With 100-s chamber nonprocessing residence and robot holding limits, increasing auxiliary inventory from four to eight wafers reduces the certified optimum from 309 to 303 s in the single-product sequential-processing case. Across 168 runs on four instances with common feasible inputs and three frozen training seeds, adding structured initialization reduces the mean makespan from 517.44 to 507.67 s for the two-product mixed instance and from 542 to 541 s for the four-product mixed instance. Improvements occur across all three training seeds. Thirty paired refinements under an extended residence window preserve makespan and reduce route waiting. The results show that joint modeling coordinates finite auxiliary-wafer reuse and full-load processing, while structured initialization and timing refinement improve feasible-solution quality and operation timing, respectively.
