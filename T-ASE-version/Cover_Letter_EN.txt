Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript "Coordinated Scheduling and Process-Mode Selection for Dual-Source Rotating-Chamber Cluster Tools" as a Regular Paper for IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering.

The manuscript addresses finite-batch automation in semiconductor equipment where product wafers and reusable dummy wafers enter from separate sources and share robots and rotating chambers. Its main contribution is a joint scheduling model of full occupancy, complete processing chains, cleaning, reusable inventory and resource timing, followed by makespan-constrained waiting and cadence refinement.

The computational evaluation separates conditional process-mode comparisons from frozen algorithm benchmarks. It includes numerical comparisons with configured SCIP, adaptive cut scoring, HEM and component ablations. The manuscript reports the limits of the evidence: the learned selector is not statistically established as superior to HEM, initial schedules are precomputed in the solver comparison, and the experiments do not demonstrate physical recipe equivalence or production deployment.
